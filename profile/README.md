## Loft by Incremental

**[Incremental-AS/loft](https://github.com/Incremental-AS/loft)** · Your NAS files on every device, kept at home.

Loft puts the shared folders on your NAS in the file app you already use. A small gateway runs at home, next to the NAS. There is no Loft account and no Loft server, and no one else stores your files.

| | |
|---|---|
| Mac | Out now |
| iPhone | In App Store review |
| Android | Coming to Google Play |
| Gateway | Debian/Ubuntu, or as a container: `ghcr.io/incremental-as/loft-gateway` |

Free, open source, Apache-2.0. Downloads and setup: [loft.incremental.no](https://loft.incremental.no)
